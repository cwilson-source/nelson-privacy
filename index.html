Nelson Tech App Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

The Nelson Tech App is published by Nelson Propane Gas Inc. in Malakoff, Texas, for use by Nelson Propane staff only. Our employees and technicians use it to complete leak check, cathodic protection, cylinder recertification, creep test, and odometer forms.

What the app collects

  • The form information a staff member enters, such as customer name, service address, account number, test readings, notes, signatures, and photos attached to the form.
  • The name of the staff member completing the form.

The app does not collect information from the general public, does not use advertising, and does not track users across other apps or websites.

How the information is used

Completed forms go to Nelson Propane's own secure servers. They are filed in the customer's account in our business records so we can meet safety and regulatory requirements. Customer lookup in the app requires a staff PIN.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent any information. We share form records only with the service providers that host our systems, and with regulators or authorities when the law requires it.

Storage and security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and kept on access-controlled systems. Forms saved on a device while offline are uploaded the next time the device connects.

Retention

Form records are kept as long as safety and regulatory rules require.
